## Full description (4000 chars max)

    LetterMyComic is a complete comic-book lettering studio. Word balloons that
    behave like ink, display lettering with real warps, 600+ comic fonts, and
    pages that come out print-ready — on a tablet, with a pen, or on any
    computer.

    WORD BALLOONS THAT BEHAVE LIKE INK
    • Speech, thought, shout, burst, whisper, captions and hand-sketched
      custom shapes — every balloon carries a pointed tail you aim with a drag
    • Drop one balloon onto another and they join automatically, connected by
      an open band the way hand-inked dialogue chains have always been drawn
    • Balloons auto-fit dialogue as you type; saved styles keep a whole book
      consistent

    TYPE BUILT FOR COMICS
    • 600+ comic fonts plus import for your own
    • Per-word bold, italic and underline, automatic comic grammar including
      the crossbar-I rule
    • Arc and envelope warping, gradient and texture fills, brush edges, glows
    • Tuck Back slips sound effects behind the artwork — its magnetic tracer
      snaps to the art's own edges

    FROM SCRIPT TO PRINTER
    • Import a whole comic script and break it into balloons automatically
    • Real comic page sizes with bleed and safe-area guides at print resolution
    • Auto panel detection reads your drawn pages and lays panel frames over
      the gutters it finds
    • Export PNG, TIFF, PDF with crop and bleed marks, or CBZ for digital
      readers — plus lettering-only transparent overlays

    MADE FOR TABLETS AND PENS
    • Pen-first: automatic palm rejection, fingertip-size handles, pinch zoom
    • Two-page spreads on one live canvas, exactly as the printed book reads

    WORK TOGETHER
    • Share a book with your letterer or editor, drop numbered comment notes
      on any page, and run review passes until it's approved

    YOUR PAGES STAY YOURS
    • No generative AI: nothing on your page is generated, and your pages are
      never used to train anything
    • Full-resolution artwork stays on your device; work autosaves as you go

    Creating an account is free and opens the full studio in demo mode.
    Subscriptions for saving, printing and print-ready export are available at
    lettermycomic.com.
