## The Pitch

Comic lettering tools are stuck in the past. The desktop apps letterers loved
are abandoned or Mac-only, the pro route means wrestling a $600 design suite
that was never built for balloons, and the "easy" web tools can't produce a
page a printer will accept.

**LetterMyComic** is a full comic lettering studio that runs entirely in your
browser. No installs, no subscriptions to a suite you use 10% of, no uploading
your unpublished pages to someone's cloud. Open a tab, drop in your art,
letter your book, export print-ready pages.

We built it the way hand-inked lettering actually works — studied from real
letterers' work, not from a clip-art library.

## Feature Tour

### Word balloons that behave like ink

- **A full balloon family** — speech, whisper (dashed), thought, double-outline,
  shout bursts, rough and sketchy pen styles, buzz, TV/radio, cosmic dot-border,
  emitter rings, captions, rounded boxes, and pill "extend" balloons.
- **Draw your own balloon** — sketch any outline by hand and it becomes a real
  balloon with a spliced tail, speech or thought style.
- **Joined balloons done right.** Connected bubbles share an **open connector**
  — no stroke crossing the junction, exactly like hand-inked lettering. Bands
  stay slim and parallel, curve smoothly as you drag, and you can tilt the
  connector's angle with a dedicated axis control.
- **Join in any order.** Drop a tail tip inside another bubble to link them —
  chains, stars, a third bubble hung off an existing pair. Overlap two joined
  bubbles and they melt into one shape; pull them apart and the band returns.
- **Styles flow across joins.** A gradient spans the whole joined group as one
  sweep — no restart seam where bubbles meet.
- **Speaker tails with craft** — aim the tip, bend the curve, keep the point on
  the character while the connector falls behind it.
- **Auto-fit** — balloons grow to hold their text as you type, and can snap to
  fit on command.

### Lettering and SFX

- **Hundreds of comic fonts** (600+ font files), organized by genre — horror
  drips, sci-fi chrome, romance scripts, classic hand-lettering — all loaded
  locally.
- **100+ ready lettering styles** and 40+ balloon styles, plus your own saved
  styles and presets.
- **Display-quality SFX**: multi-stop gradient fills, outlines, glows,
  drop shadows, and dry-media **brush textures** masked into the letterforms.
- **Warp your letters** — arc and bend layouts, plus a full 8-point envelope
  warp (corners pin, edges bow) rendered identically on screen and in print.
- **Inline emphasis** — bold/italic runs inside a balloon, like a real script.
- **The smart crossbar-I** — barred capital I only where comics grammar wants
  it (I, I'm, I'll), automatically.
- **Manipulation boxes hug the letters**, so selection and handles sit right
  on the ink — no chasing handles across the page.

### Tuck Behind Art

Make the artwork overlap your lettering — the letterer's clipping-mask trick,
automated:

- **Trace mode** — outline the foreground shape and it becomes a crisp cutout
  layered over the SFX.
- **Auto mode** — on-device AI segmentation (MobileSAM running in your
  browser) finds the figure for you.
- **Level mode** — a luminance cut for flat, high-contrast inks.
- **Opacity workflow** — knock the letters back, line up the cutout against
  the art, snap back to full opacity from the format bar.

### Pages, panels and layout

- **Standard comic dimensions** built in — trim, bleed, and live margin
  guides at real print sizes, plus an oversize "letter big, print sharp"
  preset.
- **Layout gallery** — page templates that keep your balloons when applied.
- **Panels with a fills engine** — solids, gradients (with a custom gradient
  maker), halftone dots, patterns, speedlines, and paper textures.
- **Two-page spread view**, page thumbnails, and per-page navigation.
- **Pro snapping** — margins, page centre, other elements, mirror positions,
  and equal-spacing gaps, with live alignment guides and rulers.

### Script to page

- **Import your script** and get balloons placed on the right pages, styled,
  sized, and — when a character speaks twice in a row — automatically joined
  into linked bubbles.
- **Find & replace** across the whole book.
- **Proofreading** that checks your lettering the way an editor would.

### A studio, not a toy

- **Full keyboard shortcuts**, multi-select, group move/lock/delete,
  convoy dragging that keeps spacing exact.
- **Undo history, autosave**, and a project library — including offline-safe
  local storage of your artwork.
- **Smart contextual tips** that teach real lettering craft while you work,
  backed by an original lettering knowledgebase and tutorial blog.

### Export for print and web

- **PNG / TIFF at print DPI** with proper resolution tags print shops read.
- **PDF with crop and bleed marks.**
- **CBZ** for digital readers.
- **Lettering-only export** — transparent overlays to hand back to the artist
  or production, exactly registered to the page.

## The Story

LetterMyComic started as a love letter to the desktop lettering tools that
defined an era — and a refusal to accept that their disappearance means
letterers must rent a general-purpose design suite forever. We rebuilt the
workflow from the ink up: how connectors open into both balloons, how a tail
keeps pointing at the speaker, how a barred I is only for the pronoun. The
kind of details you only get by studying real lettered pages.
